# ERVS Empirical Validation Protocol
**Experimental Blueprint for Cryo-ET and Microfluidic Shear Testing**

## 1. Objective
To empirically capture and measure the stress-triggered phase-locking of ribosomes along the outer nuclear envelope of mammalian cells subjected to controlled hydrodynamic shear stress.

## 2. Required Equipment & Reagents
* **Microfluidic Cell Culture Chambers:** PDMS-based channels capable of generating laminar shear stress gradients up to 10 MPa.
* **Cell Line:** HeLa or mammalian fibroblast lines expressing GFP-tagged ribosomal proteins (e.g., RPL5 or RPL10a).
* **Imaging Modality:** High-speed Confocal Laser Scanning Microscopy coupled with Fluorescence Recovery After Photobleaching (FRAP) and correlative Cryo-Electron Tomography (Cryo-ET).

## 3. Step-by-Step Experimental Procedure
1. **Baseline Imaging:** Seed mammalian cells in the microfluidic channel under static conditions. Record baseline ribosomal distribution (diffuse cytoplasmic vs. baseline perinuclear).
2. **Shear Application:** Introduce a rapid fluid pulse to ramp shear stress past the critical $L_{th}$ threshold ($>4.5 \text{ MPa}$) within a 500-millisecond window.
3. **Real-Time Tracking:** Monitor fluorescence intensity spikes along the outer nuclear membrane. Measure the onset timing of paracrystalline lattice compaction.
4. **Hysteresis Recovery Measurement:** Cessate the shear vector and track the 45–90 second chaperone-mediated (HSP70) un-stacking and return to translational diffusion.
5. **Genomic Integrity Assay:** Run parallel Comet Assays to quantify reduction in nuclear envelope rupture and DNA fragmentation compared to non-shunt controls.
